# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
Warden community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
gender identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing views and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or
themes
- Harassment of any kind, including but not limited to: insulting or
contemptuous remarks related to identity or background, sexual
assault, threats of violence, or intimidation
- Disrespectful behaviors towards others or their time and attention; overly
persistent personal arguments and antagonization
- Any other disruptive, insulting, or disrespectful behavior that creates a
hostile or uncomfortable environment

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers directly. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that
is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainters are
obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an
incident.
